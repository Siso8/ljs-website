Consultant|Creator|Connector

Founded in London in 2018 by Said Bayoumy, The London Consultant is an international hospitality development firm for luxury restaurants, hotels and lifestyle destinations.

We take a destination from first idea to a world-class, profitable operation. We select the brands, build the operation and stay to protect its value.

Active across the Middle East, Europe and Asia: Saudi Arabia, the UAE, Qatar, Lebanon, Egypt, the United Kingdom, Austria and Vietnam.

How We Work

One firm.Three roles.

Most destinations need a strategist, a creative studio and a well-connected operator. We are all three, under one roof.

Consultant

Feasibility, brand selection, business plans and investor advisory. Clear answers on what will work before capital is committed.

Creator

Original concepts and home-grown flagship brands, designed with our partners from first sketch to opening night.

Connector

Founder-level relationships that bring international brands into new Gulf markets, and the right operators to the right assets.
