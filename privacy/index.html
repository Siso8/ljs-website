LJ'S BURGER & CHICKEN

Privacy policy

Last updated: 18 November 2026

This policy explains what the LJ's Burger & Chicken app (iPhone app and ljsburger.com/app) collects, why, and the choices you have. You can browse the menu without an account. We only ask you to sign in to order, collect points, or claim offers.

What we collect

  • Your account: a member number, your first name, and the sign-in ID from Sign in with Apple (or the phone number you sign in with). If Apple shares it, your email address. You can choose to hide your email from us.
  • Your mobile number (optional): only if you add it, so we can reach you about an order.
  • Offers you claim: which offer, your personal code, when it was used, and an ID for your phone. This is how we make sure each offer works once per account and once per phone.
  • Notifications: if you allow notifications, a notification token for your phone and your phone's language (English or Arabic).

Your points, order history and messages are stored on your phone. We do not collect your location, contacts, photos, or browsing activity, and we do not track you across other apps or websites. We do not sell your data and we do not show ads.

Why we use it

  • To run your account, your LJ's Club card and your orders.
  • To give you offers and stop the same offer being used twice.
  • To send notifications you agreed to (order updates and offers). You can turn them off any time in iPhone Settings > LJ's > Notifications.

Who helps us

  • Cloudflare runs our app server. Account and offer data is stored in the European Union.
  • Google Firebase delivers push notifications.
  • Apple provides Sign in with Apple.

They process data only to provide these services to us.

How long we keep it

We keep your account until you delete it. When you delete your account (Me > Delete account), your name, phone number and email are removed straight away. We keep a record that an offer was used, linked only to an anonymous ID, so that offers can't be claimed twice by deleting and re-joining.

Your choices

  • Use the app without an account.
  • Hide your email with Sign in with Apple.
  • Turn notifications on or off.
  • Delete your account in the app at any time.
  • Ask us for a copy of your data or to correct it: said@ljsburger.com

Children

The app is not aimed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Changes and contact

If we change this policy we will update this page and the date above. Questions: said@ljsburger.com
