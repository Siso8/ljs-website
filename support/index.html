Common questions

Do I need an account?
No. You can browse the whole menu without one. Sign in to order, earn points and claim offers.

How do points work?
Spend 1 EGP, earn 1 point. Every reward costs its menu price × 10 in points. Points land when your order is complete.

Why does my offer code say "already used"?
Each offer works once per account and once per phone.

I'm not getting notifications.
iPhone Settings > LJ's > Notifications > Allow Notifications.

How do I delete my account?
In the app: Me > Delete account. Your name, number and points are removed.
