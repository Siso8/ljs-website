LJ'S BACK OF HOUSE - LOCK IT TO YOU AND SAID (Cloudflare Access, free)

WHAT THIS DOES
Anyone who opens ljsburger.com/boh gets a Cloudflare login page first.
Only the two emails you add can get in: they type their email, get a 6-digit
code by email, and they're in. The customer app (ljsburger.com/app) stays open to everyone.

BEFORE YOU START
- Log in to Said's Cloudflare account (the one that runs ljsburger.com).
- Cloudflare asks for a card when you turn on Zero Trust, even on the Free plan.
  The Free plan is 0 USD for up to 50 users; nothing is charged.
- Button names below may be slightly different if Cloudflare has changed its menus.

STEP 1 - TURN ON ZERO TRUST (once)
1. Go to dash.cloudflare.com and sign in.
2. In the left menu click "Zero Trust".
3. Team name: type  ljsburger  > Next.
4. Choose the "Free" plan > add the card > finish.

STEP 2 - CHECK THE EMAIL-CODE LOGIN IS ON
1. In Zero Trust, left menu: Settings > Authentication.
2. Under "Login methods" you should see "One-time PIN".
   If it's not there: "Add new" > "One-time PIN" > Save.

STEP 3 - PROTECT THE BACK OF HOUSE PAGE
1. Left menu: Access > Applications > "Add an application".
2. Choose "Self-hosted".
3. Application name:  LJ's Back of House
4. Session duration:  1 week  (how long you stay logged in on a device).
5. Add public hostname:
   - Domain: ljsburger.com     Path: boh
   Click "Add public hostname" again:
   - Subdomain: www   Domain: ljsburger.com   Path: boh
6. Next > Add a policy:
   - Policy name:  Owners
   - Action:  Allow
   - Include > Selector "Emails" > type your email, press Enter,
     then Said's email (said@ljsburger.com or the one he uses), press Enter.
7. Next > leave the other settings as they are > Save / Add application.

STEP 4 - TEST IT
1. Open a private window on your phone: ljsburger.com/boh
2. You should see the Cloudflare login. Type your email > "Send me a code".
3. Enter the 6-digit code from your inbox. The Back of House page opens.
4. Try a different email: it must be refused.
5. Open ljsburger.com/app: it must open normally with no login.

TO ADD OR REMOVE SOMEONE LATER
Zero Trust > Access > Applications > LJ's Back of House > Policies > Owners >
edit the email list > Save.
